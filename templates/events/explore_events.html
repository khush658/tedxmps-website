About The Topic ?
  • Breaking Barriers asks young people to question the silences, stereotypes and expectations they have inherited.
  • Barriers are not always walls. Sometimes they are labels, judgments or topics we are told not to discuss.
  • Through honest talks, we explore how ideas and open conversations can turn those barriers into doorways.
What Is TEDx> Maheshwari Public School YOUTH?
  • A student-focused platform for sharing inspiring ideas and perspective
  • Brings together innovative thinkers and passionate learners.
What Ideas Will We Explore ?
  • Identity Beyond Clothing: Why appearance does not define who we are.
  • Sexual Health Awareness: Breaking silence through education and open conversations.
  • Beyond the Report Card: Redefining success beyond grades and academic achievements.
