ABOUT THE EVENT

Ideas worth spreading, from the voices that will shape tomorrow.

TEDx Maheshwari Public School Youth brings students, mentors and thinkers onto one stage to share ideas that question what is possible. Every talk is built around a single belief: young people do not have to wait to make a difference.

Run independently by a passionate team of students and mentors, the event is a meaningful platform for ideas, inspiration and change.
