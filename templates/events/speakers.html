Voices that are Breaking Barriers

Six speakers from education, public service and media, each with a story about turning curiosity into impact.

UPSC & Political Science Educator

Neelam Bhatia

Neelam Bhatia is a UPSC and Political Science educator, content creator, and PhD scholar with a Master’s degree in Political Science and over a decade of teaching experience, including at the University of Delhi. Through Saral by Neelam, she reaches a community of 3 lakh+ Instagram followers, simplifying Indian Polity, public policy, international relations, and financial literacy. Featured on DD News and platforms like Josh Talks and the ET Education Summit, she is committed to empowering young Indians through accessible, impactful education.

Yogesh
YN

Government Officer & Academic

Dr. Yogesh Narania

Dr. Yogesh Narania is a government officer and academic from Ajmer, Rajasthan, known for his record of qualifying the UGC-NET examination 18 times across 17 subjects. With over 30 academic degrees, he has demonstrated expertise in diverse fields, including Public Administration, Political Science, Management, Education and Philosophy. He holds a PhD in Public Administration and Management from Maharshi Dayanand Saraswati University, Ajmer, and has served in the Rajasthan Accounts Service. He currently holds a senior role in the Directorate of Ayurveda, Government of Rajasthan. His journey reflects a commitment to lifelong learning and academic excellence.

Gaurav
GM

Wedding Anchor & Emcee

Gaurav Mathur

Gaurav Mathur is a professional wedding anchor and emcee with a diverse background in music, sports, and corporate leadership. A former HR professional with experience at startups like Josh Talks and Thrillophilia, he began his anchoring career in 2015 and has hosted 2,000+ luxury weddings, destination events, and corporate shows across India and internationally. He was honoured with the “Most Versatile Wedding Anchor of the Year” award by WIVA Awards. His journey inspires young people to explore their passions and turn them into meaningful careers.

PS

Overseas Education Counsellor

Pawan Solanki

Pawan Solanki is a seasoned overseas education counsellor with over 24 years of experience, helping students secure admissions to top global universities with scholarships. A Gold Medalist in Post Graduate Management, he is an award-winning education expert, TEDx Youth speaker, and author of Study Abroad Playbook. Recognised for his leadership and contributions to overseas education, he mentors students in making informed decisions about their academic futures.

Sorabh
PS

Overseas Education Counsellor

Sorabh Jain

Advocate Sorabh Jain has nine years of legal practice experience at the Sessions Court, Ajmer, and the Rajasthan High Court. His practice primarily focuses on criminal law, particularly cheque-dishonour cases under the Negotiable Instruments Act and cyber fraud. He holds an LL.B. from the University of Rajasthan. With over eleven years of experience mentoring students preparing for law entrance examinations, he has guided numerous aspiring legal professionals. Many of his former students now practise before the High Courts and the Supreme Court, while others have established careers in corporate law and related fields. Several have also secured selection in the judicial services. Beyond his legal practice and academic mentorship, Advocate Jain has a keen interest in geopolitics and academic writing. He regularly analyses contemporary legal and global developments, including judicial judgments, legislative changes, public protests, and international disputes, offering insights into their broader implications and significance.

Srivastava
PS

Overseas Education Counsellor

Dr.R.K.Srivastava

Dr. Srivastava is a life member of the Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT) and has presented various research papers.He served successfully as the City Coordinator for Ajmer for various examinations for more than a decade.He is a CBSE-ISTM-certified Resource Person for Capacity Building Programmes under the CBSE training unit.He has completed eight online courses (MOOCs) from IIT Bombay and earned Honour Code Certificates. Dr. Srivastava has received various awards and accolades throughout his career. He has been serving as the Principal of Maheshwari Public School, Ajmer, which has over 3,500 students, for the past 13 years. Dr. Srivastava believes in an education system that equips children with both roots and wings, preparing them to be physically fit, mentally alert, emotionally mature, and spiritually awakened. He is an acclaimed academician, a strict disciplinarian, and a result-oriented professional. His educational philosophy emphasizes learning to learn, learning to live together, wisdom-seeking learning, and lifelong learning.
