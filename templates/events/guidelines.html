06SPEAKER RULES
01SHARED VISION
01

Our Purpose

TEDx MPS is committed to creating a platform where ideas are shared, perspectives are challenged, and meaningful conversations begin. Guided by the TEDx philosophy of ideas worth spreading, we aim to encourage curiosity, creativity, critical thinking, and intellectual exploration.

02

Speaker Guidelines

RULE 2.1

Ideas over promotion

Talks must focus on a meaningful idea rather than promoting a business, product, service, or personal brand.

RULE 2.2

Originality and authenticity

Speakers should present well-developed ideas supported by credible information, relevant research, and genuine insights.

RULE 2.3

Respectful expression

Talks must avoid hate speech, discriminatory rhetoric, inflammatory advocacy, and attacks on individuals or groups.

RULE 2.4

Evidence-based claims

Scientific, medical, statistical, and other factual claims should be accurate, appropriately supported, and presented responsibly.

RULE 2.5

Time and preparation

Speakers must follow the event's assigned time limits and participate in the required rehearsals.

RULE 2.6

Presentation materials

Images, videos, music, quotations, and other materials must comply with applicable copyright requirements.

03

Content Standards

All talks and performances must uphold the principles of intellectual integrity, accuracy, respect, and responsible communication.

Content must not be used to promote commercial interests, partisan political agendas, unsupported scientific claims, or harmful misinformation. Personal experiences and opinions should be distinguished from established facts.

04

Event Conduct

01

Professionalism

All participants must maintain professionalism and respect toward speakers, guests, volunteers, and fellow team members.

02

Follow the schedule

Participants are expected to follow the event schedule and the instructions of the organising team.

03

Stage and access

Rehearsals, stage protocols, photography, recording, and backstage access must follow the permissions and procedures established by the organisers.

04

Inclusive environment

Every participant is responsible for contributing to a welcoming, disciplined, and inclusive environment.

05

Branding & Intellectual Property

The TEDx name, logo, and visual identity must be used in accordance with the official TEDx branding and licensing requirements. The TEDx MPS team must ensure that its website, promotional material, and other communications follow the applicable rules.

All third-party content must be appropriately licensed or used with permission where required. The TEDx identity must not be used to imply endorsement of products, services, or organisations.

06

Independence & Integrity

TEDx events are independently organised under a licence granted by TED. Speakers, sponsors, and external organisations must not influence the editorial integrity of the programme. The selection and presentation of ideas must remain consistent with TEDx principles.

07

Compliance

Participation in TEDx MPS requires adherence to the event's established procedures and applicable TEDx requirements. The organising team reserves the right to review content and address concerns in accordance with these requirements.

For the complete and authoritative set of rules, participants should consult the official TEDx guidelines.
